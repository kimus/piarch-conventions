This is a FULL TECHNICAL PROPOSAL review (G1), NOT an idea review. Examine the proposed design and architecture, feasibility, dependencies and environment, data/control flow, compatibility with living specs, security and trust boundaries, failure/recovery behavior, and verification strategy. Treat unresolved technical decisions that prevent safe implementation as blocking. Distinguish explicit non-goals from missing required design. Detailed normative requirements belong in a capability delta, and acceptance criteria in a deliverable contract; do not demand either in the proposal. Judge the proposal for design completeness, not for per-requirement scenarios. Do not infer implementation or tests absent from the snapshot. Ready means suitable for human baseline approval, not verified implementation.
